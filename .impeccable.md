# Impeccable — project design context

## Design Context

### Users
Operators of a quantitative options-research desk: individual traders and small
desks reading dealer positioning, options flow and regime state to make
disciplined, evidence-backed decisions. They arrive with a symbol or a
market-wide question, at a desktop, during or around market hours, and they are
comparing numbers — not browsing. The product is research and decision support
only; it never routes orders.

Primary jobs to be done:
- "Where is market-wide options activity concentrating right now?" (Flow)
- "What is the positioning and structure for this one underlier?" (Options)
- "What is dealer gamma doing, live?" (Regime)

### Brand Personality
**Measured · instrumented · non-editorial.** The interface reports what it
measured and declines to editorialise. It is closer to an oscilloscope or a
seismograph than to a consumer fintech app. Confidence comes from provenance —
every figure names its source and shows when it is stale — not from persuasion.
Uncertainty (missing, stale, proxy, degraded, research-only) is part of the data
model and stays visible rather than being smoothed away.

### Aesthetic Direction
Two deliberately separate systems, defined in `docs/DESIGN.md` and
`dashboard/src/styles/tokens.css`:

- **Desk (authenticated):** dark instrument. Near-black tinted surfaces
  (`--void #08090c`), hairline rules, corner ticks instead of rounded cards,
  a single sage/phosphor accent reserved for "live / measured now", tabular
  figures throughout, green/red reserved strictly for signed quantities.
  No glow, no gradients on data marks, no glassmorphism as decoration.
- **Public paper (landing/auth):** warm off-white editorial system — `#fbfbf8`
  canvas, oversized Inter Tight headlines, Space Mono eyebrows, one orange
  accent `#ff5229`, navy bands. Scoped; nothing leaks into the desk `:root`.

Anti-references: consumer finance apps, gaming dashboards, generic AI admin
panels, cyan-on-dark neon, hero-metric templates.

### Constraints
- **Accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA.** Contrast, keyboard navigation, focus
  visibility, ARIA on interactive elements, and form labelling are all in scope.
- **Desktop only, by design.** Phone and tablet layouts are explicitly out of
  scope; the desk assumes a large viewport. Responsive work is not a quality
  goal and should not be scored as a gap.
- Vue 3 + TypeScript + Vite, ECharts for plots, GSAP for motion.

### Design Principles
1. **Data before decoration.** The strongest object on screen is the information
   being compared.
2. **Semantics before colour.** Colour encodes state with stable meaning; it is
   never used to make empty chrome look interesting.
3. **Hierarchy before density.** Dense is allowed only when grouping, alignment
   and typography make the reading order obvious.
4. **Uncertainty stays visible.** Stale, proxy and unavailable states are
   first-class, never cosmetic edge cases.
5. **One navigation system.** Eight primary destinations; everything else lives
   in Tools. No competing sidebars, duplicate routes, or second homes.
